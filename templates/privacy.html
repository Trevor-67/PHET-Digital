Privacy Policy

This policy explains how PHET-Digital, operated by Phetego Tjie in Cape Town, South Africa, handles personal information through this website and WhatsApp.

Information you share

When you place an order on the website, we collect your name, WhatsApp number, selected package and any requirements or notes you enter. We need your name, number and package to accept an online order. The remaining details are optional. When you contact us on WhatsApp, we receive your number and the messages or files you choose to send, such as a CV for editing.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to enquiries, record and fulfil orders, and communicate with you about your service. If you submit feedback, we store it for review and may publish the approved feedback with your chosen display name or as Anonymous, with your permission.

Service providers and storage

Website orders and submitted feedback are stored in our application database. WhatsApp messages are handled through WhatsApp and Meta. Hosting and connectivity providers may process information needed to deliver the website, such as request and connection details. These providers may process information outside South Africa.

Retention and requests

The website does not automatically delete submitted orders or feedback. Contact us to ask about the information we hold, request access or correction, or request deletion where applicable. If you gave permission to publish a testimonial, you can ask us to remove it.

Contact and complaints

For a privacy question or request, contact PHET-Digital on WhatsApp at {{ whatsapp_number }}. You can also contact the Information Regulator of South Africa about a privacy complaint.
